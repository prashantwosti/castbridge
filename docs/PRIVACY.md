# CastBridge privacy policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

CastBridge mirrors your Android phone's screen and sound to your Samsung TV over your own home
network. It's made by Prashant Wosti.

## What CastBridge collects

**Nothing.** CastBridge has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own.
No personal information is collected, stored on a server, sold or shared.

## How your data moves

- While you cast, your phone's screen and sound go **directly from your phone to your TV** over
  your local Wi-Fi network. They don't pass through the internet or any third party, and they
  are not recorded.
- The phone app finds your TV on your local network and asks it to open the CastBridge TV app.
- The TV app finds your phone on your local network.

## What's stored on your devices

- **TV app:** the local network address of the last phone it connected to, so it reconnects
  faster. This stays on the TV and is deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **Phone app:** the local network addresses of Samsung TVs it has found, so it finds them again
  quickly, and, if the TV ever asked you to allow CastBridge, the pairing token it issued. These
  stay on the phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Phone permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Screen capture | To show your screen on the TV. Android asks you each time you start casting. |
| Record audio | To send the sound your apps play to the TV. CastBridge doesn't use the microphone. |
| Notifications | To show the "Casting to <your TV>" notification with a Stop button. |
| Local network / Wi-Fi | To find your TV and stream to it. |

## Links

The TV app shows a QR code that links to the phone app download on GitHub. Opening that link
is subject to GitHub's own privacy policy.

## Children

CastBridge doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
